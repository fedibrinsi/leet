Leet (title)

To be leet… or not to be leet? That is the question.

Somewhere deep within the fractured realities of the FST multiverse, a single being stands outside the flow of time. Not a student. Not a teacher. Something… older.

They call him the Creator of Timelines — the one who decides which exams happen, which deadlines vanish, and which students repeat the semester forever.

Legends say he left a trace of his power hidden inside this world’s systems — a digital whisper buried where no ordinary user would think to look.

Find him.

Find what he left behind.

And you may just break the loop… and return to the real world.
